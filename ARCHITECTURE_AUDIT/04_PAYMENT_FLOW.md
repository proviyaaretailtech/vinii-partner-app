# 04 — Payment flow

**Scope.** `vinii_partner/` at `da0a823`. Evidence labels are as in `01_DATABASE.md`.

**The app never inserts or updates `payments` itself.** It reads `payments(amount)`, embedded in `orders`, to work out what has been paid (`live_pos.dart:24,301-306`).

Payments are written in two places:

| Path | Writer | Exists? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Phone POS (counter) | RPC `pos_record_payment` | **No.** It is not defined in any migration (01 §6) |
| One Local delivery order, "Delivered" | One Local server → `ol_partner_act(…,'delivered')` | **Yes** ([ledger] OL-ui 0023) |
| Guest online payment (Cashfree) | A server seam, `GuestPayments` | **Not wired.** `PosGuestGateway.payments` returns `null` (`application/guest_gateway.dart:36`) |

## 1. Phone POS payment

**Screen:** `presentation/pos/payment_card.dart`, inside `pos_order_screen.dart`.

**User action**
- Choose Cash, UPI, Card or Split.
- Enter an amount. For cash, also enter what the guest handed over. For UPI or card, optionally a reference.
- Tap Charge.

**Code**
- `_charge` (`payment_card.dart:184-225`)
- → `PendingKeys(prefs).run('payment:<orderId>', …)` (`application/sell_keys.dart`)
- → `LivePos.recordPayment` (`live_pos.dart:141-178`)

**RPC**
- `pos_record_payment(p {order_id, method, amount, idempotency_key, reference, expected_updated_at})`.
- `method` is one of `cash`, `upi`, `card` (`domain/pos_order.dart:51-59`).
- `expected_updated_at` is the order's `updated_at` as last read.

**Client checks:** amount > 0 (`live_pos.dart:149-154`). On a single payment, `_singleProblem` (`payment_card.dart:147-160`) also blocks an amount above what is due, and cash received below the amount.

**Expected response:** the order, including `paid` (sum of payments) and `status`.

**Actual outcome today:** "Not available yet": *"Recording payments from the phone needs the POS payment function … Settle this bill at the POS counter."* The payment key stays saved.

### Payment methods
- Offered by the phone: `cash`, `upi`, `card`.
- Allowed by the database (`payments.method`): `cash`, `upi`, `card`, `wallet`, `due`, `other`.
- Allowed on `orders.payment_method`: `cash`, `upi`, `card`, `split`.

### Split payments
- **Code:** `_chargeSplit` (`payment_card.dart:227-262`) and `_secondPart` (`:265-289`).
- **How it works:**
  - The two parts must add up exactly to what is due. Short or over blocks the button.
  - Each part is a **separate `pos_record_payment` call** with its own saved key: `payment:<orderId>` and `payment:<orderId>:2`.
  - If part 2 fails, part 1 stays recorded, and the card offers "try the second part again" (`_retrySecondPart`, `:291-303`).
- In the database a split is simply several `payments` rows. `orders.payment_method = 'split'` exists, but this app never sends it.

### Payment status
- **There is no payment-status column** on `orders` or `payments`.
- The phone derives it from the order:
  - `paid` = the RPC's `paid`, otherwise the sum of `payments.amount`.
  - `due` = `total − paid`.
  - `isPaid` = `status == paid`, or `due ≤ 0.009` (`domain/pos_order.dart:228`).

### Transaction IDs
- **Database side:** `payments.id` (uuid, generated by the database) and `payments.reference` (free text, **not unique**).
- **Phone side:** the idempotency key (UUID v4) is generated on the phone. The `payments` table has no column to store it.
- The phone shows no gateway or terminal transaction id: there is no card terminal integration.

### Cash handling
- The cash tendered and the change to give back are worked out **on the phone, for display only** (`payment_card.dart`, `cashReceived` passed to `onRecorded`).
- The cash tendered is **not sent** to the server: the RPC payload has no such field.
- There is no cash drawer, till session or shift in this app.

### Refunds and settlement
**None in this app.** It has:
- no refund screen
- no refund RPC call
- no settlement or payout logic

One Local has `ol_refunds` and `ol_process_refund` (OL-ui 0024), but this app never calls them.

## 2. One Local delivery order: cash on delivery

**Trigger:** "Delivered" (slide to confirm, `delivery_order_screen.dart:226`) → `POST /api/partner/orders/<id>/delivered {version}`.

**Server** (`ol_partner_act`, delivered branch, [ledger] OL-ui 0023)
- **Check:** `ol_orders.status` must be `out`, otherwise `OL_WRONG_STATE`.
- **UPDATE `orders`:**
  - `status = 'delivered'`
  - `payment_method = 'cash'`
  - `paid_at = now()`
  - `updated_at = now()`
- **INSERT `payments`:**
  - `method = 'cash'`
  - `amount = orders.total`, which **includes the One Local delivery fee**. `ol_create_restaurant_order` sets `total = subtotal + gst + fee`.
  - `reference = 'onelocal:<ol_orders.id>'`
  - `created_by = actor`
- **UPDATE `ol_orders`:** `status = 'delivered'`, `payment_status = 'paid'`.
- **Events:** `delivered` and `paid` ("Cash ₹… collected on delivery").

**What is not done**
- No idempotency key protects this insert. A replay is blocked only by the state check (`out` is required), plus the `version` match.
- The final status is `delivered`, not `paid`.
- The partner API always reports `payment: 'Cash on delivery'` (`restaurant.ts`, `shape()`).

## 3. Guest online payment (Cashfree): present in code, not connected

- `application/guest_payments.dart` defines the flow. The server would create the gateway order and later report the verified outcome.
- `data/payments/cashfree_checkout_web.dart` loads Cashfree's JS SDK from its CDN and opens the checkout modal (sandbox by default).
- Non-web builds throw "Paying online opens in the web version" (`cashfree_checkout_stub.dart`).
- `GuestOrderGateway.payments` defaults to `null`, and `PosGuestGateway` does not override it. **No online payment can start from any shipped gateway.** The guest bill tells the guest to pay staff.
